## Selected Work

**Generative Media**
- **vorch** *(proprietary)*: the pipeline behind our AI ad production, covering product stills, video ads and narrated spots, with model self-evaluation.
- **TrainGen**: narrated, bilingual training videos generated from a live product UI.

**Speech AI**
- **Bulgarian ASR**: speech recognition model trained from scratch on 2,200+ h of Bulgarian; 3.93% WER on unseen parliament sessions, plus a streaming version (~640 ms latency) that holds up on phone-line audio. [Demo](https://asr.cdsv.dev)
- **Bulgarian TTS**: Bulgarian layer for the open-source OmniVoice TTS, with native accent, correct lexical stress and a cloned narrator voice; it voices vorch and TrainGen.
- **Voice AI Receptionist**: Bulgarian-speaking voice agent that takes calls and books appointments.

**Agentic Systems & RAG**
- **Tourism AI Platform** *(private)*: LangGraph agents, RAG and PostGIS routing for travel in Bulgaria. [Live](https://thewonderfulbulgaria.com/)
- **AI Code Reviewer**: multi-agent pull-request review integrated with GitHub Actions.

**Resilient Sensing & Geospatial**
- **STOZHER** *(prototype)*: GNSS jamming and spoofing detection with signed reports over satellite, LoRa mesh or any network. Built at the 12th CASSINI Hackathon.

**Business Automation**
- **PaperlessAI**: invoice extraction from PDFs and scans into structured accounting data. [Demo](https://paperlessai.app)
- **Compliance Hub** *(private)*: AI-assisted ISO 45001 compliance management. [Demo](https://compliance.cdsv.dev/)
- **Toy Recognition** *(private)*: CLIP and FAISS product identification at the point of sale. [Demo](https://toys.cdsv.dev) · [Notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1R7z3oUPwFUimsx9J5OGB1OWXw12RZALh?usp=sharing)
